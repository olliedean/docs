We’ll explain where to find, how to obtain, and how **Templates** works to craft armor & tools!

### Where & How

**AllTheModium** ores can be found across 3 dimensions: **Overworld**, **Nether**, and **The End**, normally exposed to **AIR**.

* **AllTheModium**: (Overworld) <img src="/images/allthemodium/allthemodium_ingot.png" alt="" style={{ display: "inline", verticalAlign: "middle" }} />
  * Found in [Deep Dark](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Deep_Dark) Biome, also in **Mining Dimension** Deep Slate layer (Y 65-129).
    * **Mining Dimension** is more rare, at-least 1 ore per chunk.
    * Killing **Warden** also grants a Quest reward ATM Ingot.
  * Obtained with a **Netherite** level pick or better.
* **Vibranium**: (Nether) <img src="/images/allthemodium/vibranium_ore.webp" alt="" style={{ display: "inline", verticalAlign: "middle" }} /><img src="/images/allthemodium/vibranium_ingot.png" alt="" style={{ display: "inline", verticalAlign: "middle" }} />
  * Found in **Nether** dimension (no longer biome specific).
    * Elevation between Y 64-127
  * Also Found between Y 0-40 within **The Other** dimension.
  * Obtained with an **AllTheModium** level pick or better.
* **Unobtainium**: (End) <img src="/images/allthemodium/unobtainium_ore.webp" alt="" style={{ display: "inline", verticalAlign: "middle" }} /><img src="/images/allthemodium/unobtainium_ingot.png" alt="" style={{ display: "inline", verticalAlign: "middle" }} />
  * Found within **End Highlands**.
    * More rare than other ores
  * Obtained with **Vibranium** level pick or better

Ores Can Only Be Mined By A ‘Real Player’

### Templates

* **AllTheModium**: <img src="/images/allthemodium/allthemodium_upgrade_smithing_template.png" alt="" style={{ display: "inline", verticalAlign: "middle" }} />
  * Found in **Sus Clay** in **Ancient Cities** within the **Overworld**.


* **Vibranium**: <img src="/images/allthemodium/vibranium_upgrade_smithing_template.png" alt="" style={{ display: "inline", verticalAlign: "middle" }} />
  * Found in **Sus SoulSand** in **Bastions** within the **Nether**.

<img src="/images/allthemodium/vibranium_loc.avif" alt="Vibranium location" width="600" />

* **Unobtainium**: <img src="/images/allthemodium/unobtainium_upgrade_smithing_template.png" alt="" style={{ display: "inline", verticalAlign: "middle" }} />
  * Found in **The Other** dimension, as chest loot in the underground library of `allthemodium:dungeon` structure.
